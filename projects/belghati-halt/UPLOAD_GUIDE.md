# How to upload "Belghati Halt Ke 7 Rules" to your Twist AI channel

Takes about 15 minutes. Works on a computer (easiest) or the YouTube Studio app.

## What you need (sent to you in chat)
1. **belghati_halt_FINAL.mp4** — the video (subtitles already on screen)
2. **thumbnail.png** — the cover picture
3. **youtube-description.txt** — the description with chapter times
4. **captions.srt** — subtitle file (optional extra, helps YouTube search)

Save all four to your phone/computer first.

---

## Step 1 — Make the playlist (one time only)
Name it **Horror Twist** ("horror" first is what people search). If you want extra search help,
use **Horror Twist | Hindi Horror Stories**.

1. Go to **studio.youtube.com** and make sure the top-right picture is your **Twist AI** channel
   (click the picture → *Switch account* if not).
2. Left menu → **Content** → **Playlists** tab → **New playlist**.
3. Title: `Horror Twist` · Visibility: **Public** → **Create**.

## Step 2 — Unlock custom thumbnails (one time only)
Go to **youtube.com/verify**, enter your phone number, type the code you get by SMS.
Without this, YouTube won't let you upload the thumbnail.

## Step 3 — Upload the video
1. In YouTube Studio click **Create** (top right, camera with +) → **Upload videos**.
2. Choose **belghati_halt_FINAL.mp4**. While it uploads, fill in the details:

**Title** (copy exactly):
```
Belghati Halt Ke 7 Rules | Raat 3:33 Ki Train | Hindi Horror Story Animated
```

**Description:** open `youtube-description.txt`, copy everything, paste it in.
(The chapter times inside it make YouTube show chapters automatically.)

**Thumbnail:** click **Upload file** → choose `thumbnail.png`.

**Playlists:** tick **Horror Twist**.

**Audience:** choose **"No, it's not made for kids"** (horror). Age restriction: **No**.

3. Click **Show more** and set:
   - **Altered content:** choose **Yes** (the pictures and voice are AI-made and look realistic —
     YouTube asks you to say so; it does not hurt your video).
   - **Tags** (copy):
     ```
     hindi horror story, horror story in hindi, railway horror story, bhootiya kahani, animated horror story, horror stories in hindi, night shift horror, train horror story, horror twist, rules horror story
     ```
   - **Language:** Hindi · **Category:** Film & Animation
4. Click **Next**.

## Step 4 — Subtitles (optional but good for search)
On the **Video elements** page → **Add subtitles** → language **Hindi** → **Upload file** →
**With timing** → choose `captions.srt` → **Done**.
(The subtitles are already burned on screen; this extra file only helps YouTube understand the video.)

**End screen:** skip for now (add one later when you have a second video to point to).

## Step 5 — Checks and publish
1. **Checks** page: wait until it says *No issues found* → **Next**.
2. **Visibility:** pick **Schedule** and choose **today, 8:00 PM** India time
   (evening is when horror gets watched), or **Public** to post now.
3. Click **Schedule** / **Publish**. Done!

---

## After uploading
- Watch it once on your phone to check sound and subtitles.
- Pin a comment like: *"Agar aapko pehli posting mile… kya aap register ka last page padhoge? 👀"*
  (questions get comments, comments help the video spread).
- Post the next story within a week and add it to **Horror Twist** too — channels grow from regular uploads.

## Good to know
- **Money:** YouTube pays only after the channel joins the Partner Program
  (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours in a year). Until then, focus on regular uploads.
- **ElevenLabs sound effects:** made on your free ElevenLabs plan. Check ElevenLabs' terms before
  you monetise — free-plan outputs may not be allowed for commercial use or may need credit.
- The story, images, voice (AI4Bharat Indic Parler-TTS, Apache-2.0) and music bed are free to use.
